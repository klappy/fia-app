# Audio is the player; the pages turn to the audio

The model inverts. Until now each screen restarted its own clip of the recording and the audio drove navigation only at a clip's end or inside an authored together-list. Now a recorded guide stage opens one media session for a **run**: the consecutive same-file clips in activity order from the current screen up to and including the first confirmation stop (a discussion question), or up to a file change, a screen without a recording, or a visual step whose pause cue is replaced by its description or video. The controller plays the run's outer range once and turns the page by the player clock; both pause when the audio pauses; Next, Back, swipes and the Overview seek the running session to the chosen screen and keep the play/pause state the person had.

The captain ordered this on 2026-11-09: "Make audio the player and the pages turn to the audio. Both pause if the audio pauses. Pages may jump over things, which may cause the audio to seek to the appropriate moment. This inverts the model to fix the audio sync." It applies the direction recorded on 2026-10-26 that the audio file should persist screen to screen.

Inside a run the gaps between clips are never voiced: when the clock reaches a clip's authored end and the next clip starts later, the player seeks to that start, so the spoken question numbers and the "I will pause the audio here" cues that live in those gaps stay unheard whatever the decoder clock does. Only the run's last span carries the measured clip tail. A page turn that arrives more than 250 ms late re-anchors the audio at the span start. A confirmation stop ends the run at its own end and waits for Continue. The authored together-lists keep their own behaviour (no seeks inside their approved outer bounds).

The planner is the safety gate. Every span keeps the identity, source-pin, mapping and verified-timing checks the together planner already applied; a run that cannot be planned (one clip, an unverified descriptor) returns null and the screen plays its clip on its own exactly as before. Byte identity, source pins and range validation did not relax.

With automatic narration off, a manual Play opens the same session and every row the clock crosses is completed, so progress totals are right; Continue seeks forward within the run. Returning to a page that was hidden re-syncs the screen and the highlight to the player clock, and the screen stays awake while narrating where the platform allows it. No control, row, link or marker was added or changed on the stage; the gel looks the same.

Evidence is synthetic-clock: the real App on the shipped contracts, descriptors and sidecars, with the player clock driven by the tests. It does not certify acoustic synchronization on a device; that remains the physical listening pass.
