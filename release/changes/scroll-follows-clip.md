# Reading follows the clip being spoken

Untimed reading (guide units and passage-only Scripture) now follows the clip's own clock, `progressElapsed` over `progressDuration`, instead of the shared recording file's. Before, a guide clip cut from minutes into one recording opened with the text jumping 62–92% down within 500 ms of Play; the first spoken line left the screen. Now the first line holds in the clear reading area for the first 10% of the clip, and the text moves linearly to the end by 90%. Whole-file playback keeps elapsed over duration.

Word-timed Scripture keeps absolute media time for alignment lookups. Inside a gap between words, the target stays on the last spoken word's line instead of re-centring on the verse paragraph, so the view no longer steps back about 11 px at each gap. Between verses, the previous verse stays marked until the next verse starts. The smooth-follow constant and reduced-motion behaviour are unchanged.

Validation: unit tests cover the S02-U010 range (start 304.51 s of a 498.77 s file), the 90% end point, the four measured word gaps on the delivered BSB Mark 1:1–13 alignment and the verse hold; component tests cover guide, passage-only and word-gap targets. `e2e/scroll-follow.spec.js` mirrors scripted journey J4 on Mark 1:1–13 S02-U010, S02-U013 and BSB against the local build. Before the change the guide screens moved 1004 px and 992 px in the first 500 ms after `playing`; after it they move 0 px, and BSB shows 0 reversals instead of 4. Passage-only Mark 1:14–20 BSB is not served by the local build, so its J4 check belongs to the DEV post-deploy run.
