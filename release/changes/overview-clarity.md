# The overview reads at a glance: where you are, the six stages, everything else one tap away

The overview sheet is recomposed as one hierarchy, each level smaller than the one above. First a here-card, the largest thing on the sheet: `You are here · step N of 6`, the stage icon and name in the title size, `item i of n`, and one line `Now: <kind> · then <kind>`. Then the six stages as the spine: icon + name with the item count at the end; done stages muted with a check, stages ahead muted, the current one on a soft fill. Only the current stage shows its items, as one row of small circles (done filled, current ringed, ahead outlined, an upcoming discussion ringed), each a 48 px control with its kind word for assistive tech, and one caption line `7 done · discuss now · 3 to go`. Any other stage's items open in place when its row is tapped; tapping an item navigates, and a jump never marks skipped items complete. Last, three quiet rows: `What the icons mean` (collapsed by default; Content — listen, Scripture, discuss, key term, picture, map, video — and The big button — Play, Continue, Discuss, then continue, Finish), `Language`, `Passages`, then Close in its sticky footer.

Removed from the default view: the standing Key block, the per-item word under every tile, the thumbnails in the mini-map (items are kind icons in circles), and the chevron rows at reading size. The six stage icons are not repeated in the key (the spine shows each beside its name).
