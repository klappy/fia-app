# Verified visual publication and screen binding

A reviewed visual descriptor can now be served through a fixed same-origin route after its current presentation, source evidence, retained bytes and decoded-pixel evidence are verified. Service-worker status, explicit viewing and offline download preserve the same descriptor. The screen binds that descriptor to its authored image or map, including a cold offline reload; publisher URLs and pending metadata do not become display paths.

The reviewed publication registry remains empty. This change admits no new publisher assets and does not establish whole-corpus media completeness. Acquisition blocked by the outbound proxy was not retried through another route or environment.

An offline video helper verifies actual prebuilt bytes against existing rights/review/replacement ledgers and requires explicit selection between bundled logical bytes and the qualified derivative. The helper does not change a selected delivery or grant publisher acquisition.

Validation uses bounded fixture bytes, real worker/SW custody paths and App online/offline regressions. Physical-device, human Spanish and audible whole-corpus acceptance remain separate gates. The previously accepted passage-switch disposition is unchanged.
