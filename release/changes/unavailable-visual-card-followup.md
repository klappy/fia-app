# Unavailable picture or map: the status card keeps its aspect and never scrolls

Follow-up to `unavailable-visual-stage.md` (#321).
- The bounded status card takes the record's own aspect when the record carries width and height, and keeps the 4:3 placeholder otherwise. Under the height cap it keeps that aspect instead of stretching wide.
- On short phones and at large text the card is floored at its own text (two lines plus padding where it keeps its aspect, `calc(2lh + 40px)` with a `calc(3em + 40px)` fallback; its text height on stages 680px tall or less), so it never becomes a small scrolling region or an unnamed keyboard stop. The step words keep their floor and stay first. When a desktop stage is short of room (for example 1280×700 at reading scale 1.5), the card gives up its aspect height before the words give up their floor, so it still ends above the controls.
- On short stages (680px tall or less) the card drops the placeholder aspect (`aspect-ratio: auto`), shrinks to its text, and spans the stage width less a 52px gutter (at most 676px), so it stays a short band above the bottom controls. A loading, canceled or failed picture's Cancel or Try again button sits beside the text with a 44px target on these stages, and the card's margins tighten under a raised stage notice, so the interactive card also stays above the controls (the buddy review had measured 11px into the controls at 390×560 with a notice raised, before this follow-up).
- **Disclosed for design acceptance:** on very short stages (480px tall or less, for example a landscape phone at 844×390 with the chrome showing) the step words' floor (design-lens rule R8) relaxes from `min(4lh, 40%)` to `min(2lh, 25%)`. At 844×390 that is about 46px, roughly 1.3 lines; the words still scroll and take keyboard focus. The card tightens to one compact line. Each `lh` floor has a non-`lh` fallback before it (`min(6.4em, 40%)`, `min(3.2em, 25%)`).
- **Disclosed for design acceptance:** on those very short stages, while a stage notice is raised (#330, about 70px more of the top band), the words drop their floor entirely (`min-height: 0`; they still scroll and take keyboard focus) and the card's margins tighten, so the card stays above the controls. On these stages the picture's title and credit also drop their 52px minimum height and spacing, which gives the words back about 30px. Measured on the loopback preview at 844×390: without a notice the words get about 49px (1.3 lines); with the narration-blocked notice raised they collapse to 0px until the notice is dismissed (they remain a focusable, scrollable region), and the card sits 31px above the controls. Found in the design-packet capture at 844×390 with the narration-blocked notice, where the card had sat under the controls.
- On a coarse-pointer landscape phone, a picture or map step still opens the existing immersive view (chrome and controls hidden) until it is dismissed; the 844×390 rule covers the dismissed and desktop-window cases.

Tests: `apps/web/tests/media-stage.spec.js` (record aspect, no tabindex, the shipped card rule can never scroll, `lh` fallbacks present), `e2e/visual.spec.js` (`manual-download-required`: glass ground, aspect, bounded height, words floor, card inside the stage above the controls, no scrolling card and Tab skipping the card at 390×640, 390×560, 390×640 at reading scale 1.5, and 844×390 at reading scale 1 and 1.5, including the card staying above the controls with a raised notice at 844×390, and, at every stage 680px tall or less, the interactive card with a Try again button, with and without a raised notice; at 1280×800 and 1280×700, each at reading scale 1 and 1.5, the card ends inside the stage and does not scroll).
