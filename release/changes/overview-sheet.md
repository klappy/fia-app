# Tapping the bar opens the overview: key, Language, Passages, then the mini-map

Tapping the session bar now opens the overview sheet, the navigation home. Its order is fixed: an icon key (the six stage icons with their stage names, then the content icons with their words: listen, Scripture, discuss, key term, picture, map, video), an intro slot that is absent until Word Collective content exists, a Language row, a Passages row, the mini-map, and Close. The Language and Passages rows show the current value and open the Language and Passages pickers inline; a refused passage open shows its reason on its card and the sheet stays open. The mini-map is the existing overview (stage headings, thumbnails where a screen has one, icons otherwise) and navigates as before; every item now carries its kind word under it (listen, Scripture, discuss, key term, picture, map, video), one line, truncated, in a hit box of at least 48 px. The sheet is titled Overview, as in the mock; its header is opaque over scrolled content, and Close is the primary button in a padded footer band that stays in reach while the sheet scrolls.
