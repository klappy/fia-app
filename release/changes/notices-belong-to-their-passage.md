# Notices belong to the passage and screen that raised them

A notice now records where it was raised: its passage and revision, its screen, the sheet that was open, and its kind (error, unavailable or info). It ends with that scope. Opening a passage clears the previous passage's notices before the new heading renders, so a Spanish RV1909 "No recording is available…" no longer sits on the first screen of English Mark 1:14–20, and a refused switch leaves no stale error on the next successful one. A screen's notice also ends when that screen changes, and a notice raised while nothing was playing ends once playback starts, so "Tap Play to hear…" clears on Resume.

A notice raised while a sheet is open shows inside that sheet, under its title row, instead of behind the modal; it ends when the sheet closes. A notice the reading screen raised stays with the reading screen while a sheet is open over it. The offline and preparation notices keep their own rules and only move into the sheet when they appear while it is open.

Validation: `apps/web/tests/notice-scope.spec.js` covers C1–C5 at the App boundary with the real Mark 1:21–28, Mark 1:29–34 and Marcos/Mark 1:14–20 passages; 7 of its 9 cases fail on `3d3b1d2` and all pass with the change. `apps/web/tests/notice-scope.test.js` covers the scope rules and kinds. `e2e/notice-scope.spec.js` mirrors guards G5 and G6 against the packaged Worker preview with the service worker allowed: on `3d3b1d2` the stale notice is still on screen 600–800 ms after the new heading and the in-sheet notice does not exist; with the change the stale notice never renders with the new heading, and the in-sheet notice is the element at its own centre while Close stays reachable.
