# Bounded recognition window contract

This pure planner/projector performs no decoding, recognition, acquisition or job dispatch. It does not raise the existing runtime's 600-second or byte limits. Source and decoded mono 16 kHz PCM hashes, decoder recipe, model and config hashes bind each deterministic sample-clock window. A requested source may span at most 24 hours, with at most 1,024 windows; each window is at most 600 seconds, overlap at most 30 seconds and less than half its window. These are planning bounds, not measured runtime capacity.

`planRecognitionWindows(request)` returns immutable-identity window instructions (start sample inclusive, end exclusive). The trusted caller must establish that the supplied totalSamples/PCM hash actually describe the pinned source decoded by that recipe. This module checks identity shapes, not audio bytes. No user URL or arbitrary source acquisition is involved.

`projectRecognitionWindows(plan, records)` revalidates the entire plan. Completed records carry hash-verified raw JSON bytes with schema `fia-window-raw-words@1`, the exact windowSha256 and words `{word,startSample,endSampleExclusive}` in the local window sample clock. All words retain exact text, raw artifact hash, raw word index and local/global sample offsets. Records may arrive out of order; projection is deterministically ordered. Unknown/duplicate windows, altered plans/bytes and invalid clocks refuse. Missing/uncertain windows remain explicit and never cause automatic retries.

Overlap words are preserved from both windows, even when text matches. Any words touching an overlap produce explicit unresolved reconciliation evidence; they are not silently deduplicated or declared drift. Empty overlap observations do not establish acoustic silence. `projected` means structural projection only, never a verified continuous transcript or accepted playback. All accepted-range arrays remain empty.

Integration still requires a bounded streaming decoder, truthful source-to-PCM sample-clock provenance, an executor persisting each window's attempt/uncertainty and raw artifact, controlled recognition capability and budgets, and a reviewed overlap reconciliation policy. Adapt recognizer time estimates to local samples with an explicit versioned rounding policy and retained original timings; do not silently relabel seconds. Decoder/ASR runtime, stitching decisions, quiet-boundary/native-clock qualification, factory wiring and publication are not implemented here. Consume the plan windows directly as bounded work instructions; reuse completed receipts by windowSha256, and require an external recovery decision for uncertain work. No mass work or paid authority is added.
